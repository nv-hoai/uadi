Limits of this dashboard

  • Reviewer type is not inferred here. Check the study record: LLM sessions are not independent people.
  • A machine review is screening-grade. It is not human review and does not establish human-perceived quality.
  • 11 of 126 dimension judgments remained split. Averaging is a decision rule, not consensus.
  • Visual scoring used the screenshots the reviewers captured, typically default states only.
  • All values are descriptive; no statistical test was performed.
