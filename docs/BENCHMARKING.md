# Evaluating Design Intelligence

UADI should be judged by the quality of the design outcomes it enables.

Use the paired protocol and rubric in [benchmarks/README.md](../benchmarks/README.md), starting with the [subscription settings task](../benchmarks/subscription-settings.md).

## Evaluation principles

- Compare the same agent/model and project context with and without UADI.
- Keep tool access and run settings as consistent as possible.
- Review outputs blind where practical.
- Use more than one reviewer and more than one task/run before drawing conclusions.
- Score actual outcomes, not how many workflow steps or artifacts were produced.
- Include time/effort and defects alongside quality scores.
- Preserve raw outputs and disclose what was and was not tested.

## What success means

Evidence of improvement should show stronger product understanding, UX reasoning, design judgment, visual hierarchy, interaction completeness, accessibility/responsiveness and implementation quality—without unnecessary process overhead.

Do not market benchmark results until they have been run, documented and independently reviewed.
