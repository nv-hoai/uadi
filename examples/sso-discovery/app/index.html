Require SSO for Kestrel?

Every member will need to sign in with Okta. Enforcement starts at the next sign-in, and existing sessions expire within 24 hours.

Prepare recovery first

Download a recovery link before you continue. Only Owners can use it if your identity provider is unavailable.

Turn off SSO enforcement?

Members will be able to sign in with a password again. Existing SSO sessions are not revoked by this action.

Connect identity provider

Paste the SAML metadata URL from your identity provider. This prototype does not contact a provider.
