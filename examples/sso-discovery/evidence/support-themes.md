# Support themes — workspace security

**Source:** 14 tickets tagged `security`, `sso` or `login`, January–March 2026.

**Method:** Themes were grouped by hand. Quotes and account identifiers are removed. This is a small, self-selected sample, not a representative study.

## Themes

| Theme | Tickets | What it suggests | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admins expect to manage SSO | 6 | Admins look for security settings and are surprised by "Ask an owner" | Whether Admins should be allowed to enforce SSO |
| Fear of locking everyone out | 5 | Enforcement feels high-risk; customers want a recovery path | Which recovery mechanism is acceptable |
| Uncertainty about existing sessions | 3 | Customers do not know whether enforcement signs people out immediately | What the product currently does |
| Contractor or partner domains | 2 | Some workspaces need a second identity provider | Whether multi-IdP is in scope |
| Confusion after a plan change | 2 | Customers are unsure whether SSO survives a downgrade | The actual billing and entitlement rules |

## Signals worth acting on

- The permission conflict is visible to customers, not only to support.
- Enforcement needs a consequence statement and a recovery path before the action is available.
- The label "Require SSO" is clearer than "Enable SSO" for the enforcement action.

## Limits

The sample cannot establish prevalence, willingness to pay or the correct permission model. Do not cite these counts as customer research.
